  • Your light is your life. It slowly fades — golden Lumen shards restore it.
  • Shadows drain your light on contact. Every Shadow you purge gives a little light back. Keep a combo going for bonus damage.
  • Gather every shard on a moon and a guardian boss rises. Defeat it to open the Skyward Beacon.
  • Moon folk with a ! have quests. Their rewards upgrade your Sunblade and your light.
  • Smash glowing Star Caches for items and power-ups: Haste, Fury, Aegis, Radiance and Magnet.
  • The radar maps the whole moon: you are the centre, the rim is the far side. The small golden arrow points to your next goal.
